# AgriSolar public-site content review

The development site deliberately avoids publishing the following until the owner verifies the facts and approves public use.

## Projects and photographs

- Worden site: customer/public project name, exact location wording, acreage, work dates, scope, crew, equipment, results, and permission to publish.
- Identify which repository photographs are owned by AgriSolar and which depict completed AgriSolar work.
- Supply approved before-and-after captions and image dates.

## Credentials and operating details

- Exact Illinois commercial pesticide license categories and any license number that should be public.
- Approved insurance wording and limits. Do not publish “fully insured” until confirmed.
- Current equipment actually owned and available for solar work. Do not present planned or researched equipment as part of the fleet.
- Any safety training, vendor-system registrations, or certifications that can be documented.

## Customer proof

- Customer-approved testimonials with the speaker’s approved name/title or approved anonymous attribution.
- Customer permission for logos, project names, site locations, photographs, acreage, and quantified outcomes.

## Business information

- Whether a public street address should be listed or Belleville, Illinois should remain the only office location.
- Confirm the public service-area wording: maximum 75-mile radius from Belleville, including the portions of Missouri that fall inside it.
- Decide whether honey/products content should return as a secondary footer page. It remains outside the primary commercial navigation.

## Future form data

- Facility type, service frequency, and vegetation condition are currently included in the submission message so the existing development database rules remain compatible.
- If these fields should become separate CRM fields, update and test the database schema/rules before deploying those rule changes.
