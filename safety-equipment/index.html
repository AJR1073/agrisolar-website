Safety & Equipment

Mowing around solar panels takes planning. Clearances, terrain, site access, and electrical equipment all affect how the work can be done.

Plan before work begins

Start with the site conditions

The agreed work plan should identify the areas to be serviced, the access routes, and any hazards or restrictions.

We use those details to choose mowing and vegetation-control methods for the site.

  • Panel-row and under-panel clearances
  • Gates, travel routes, and turn areas
  • Inverters, transformers, fencing, and marked utilities
  • Vegetation height, density, terrain, and ground conditions
  • Facility rules, communication contacts, and restricted areas

Choosing equipment for the site

Panel-row mowing

Panel height, row spacing, turning room, and terrain help determine whether low-profile mowing equipment is suitable.

Open areas & tight spaces

Equipment needs to suit the acreage while allowing careful work near panels and other obstacles.

Targeted herbicide work

Licensed herbicide application may be part of the plan when the vegetation, product label, weather, and facility rules allow it.

Field communication

When included in the work agreement, photos and notes keep the site contact informed about conditions and completed work.

Before crews arrive

Please share the site map, access instructions, gate dimensions, and vegetation requirements. Include known hazards, restricted areas, a site contact, and any facility safety rules.

We confirm equipment, methods, and timing for each contract. Vegetation observations are not an electrical inspection, engineering assessment, or panel repair.
