Safety & Equipment

Commercial solar sites require deliberate planning around tight clearances, changing terrain, and sensitive infrastructure.

Solar-specific planning

The site determines the method

Before work begins, the agreed scope should identify access routes, panel clearance, fence lines, equipment pads, obstacles, wet areas, debris, and known infrastructure concerns.

AgriSolar LLC selects mowing and vegetation-control methods around those conditions. The goal is controlled, repeatable work without treating a solar facility like an ordinary lawn.

  • Panel-row and under-panel clearances
  • Gates, travel routes, and turn areas
  • Inverters, transformers, fencing, and marked utilities
  • Vegetation height, density, terrain, and ground conditions
  • Facility rules, communication contacts, and restricted areas

How equipment is matched to the work

Panel-row mowing

Low-profile commercial mowing equipment is considered for row spacing, panel height, turning room, and terrain.

Large-area efficiency

Equipment choices account for acreage and production needs without ignoring constrained or sensitive areas.

Targeted control

Licensed herbicide application may complement mechanical mowing when it fits the vegetation, label, weather, and facility requirements.

Field communication

When included in the scope, photographs and observations help the designated contact understand conditions and completed work.

Before mobilization

Useful planning information includes the site map, gate dimensions, known hazards, access instructions, vegetation specifications, restricted areas, designated contacts, and any facility-specific safety requirements.

Final equipment, procedures, and scheduling are confirmed for the contracted scope. AgriSolar LLC does not present vegetation observations as electrical inspection, engineering, or solar-panel repair.
