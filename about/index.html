About AgriSolar LLC

We’re a family-operated business in Belleville, Illinois, caring for the land around solar panels.

A Background in Agriculture

Our team’s experience includes managing a 1,000-acre agricultural operation. That work taught us to pay attention to growing conditions, soil, weather, and the timing of each job.

We bring that approach to solar sites, where vegetation work also calls for care around panels, wiring, gates, and access routes. Our services cover routine upkeep and areas that need extra attention.

Team members hold commercial pesticide licenses. When herbicides are needed, applications follow product labels and the site’s requirements.

What Guides Our Work

Clear Expectations

Care for the Ground

We consider soil conditions and existing ground cover when planning mowing, planting, or treatment.

Direct Communication
