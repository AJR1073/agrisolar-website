About AgriSolar LLC

A family-operated Illinois business focused on the land and vegetation surrounding solar infrastructure.

Agricultural Experience, Applied Carefully

AgriSolar LLC is based in Belleville, Illinois. Our team’s background includes managing a 1,000-acre agricultural operation and working with large-scale land-management needs.

That experience informs how we approach access, timing, vegetation pressure, soil conditions, and communication at solar sites. Our contracted work centers on mowing, vegetation control, native planting, erosion-control support, and ongoing site maintenance.

Team members hold commercial pesticide licensing. Herbicide applications are planned for targeted vegetation needs and performed in accordance with product labels and site requirements.

How We Work

Understand the Scope

We start with the site location, acreage, access, current vegetation conditions, schedule, and requested services.

Plan Around the Site

Mowing, planting, targeted treatment, and stabilization work are considered together when the scope calls for it.
