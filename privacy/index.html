Privacy Policy

How information submitted through this website is used.

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, the form may collect your name, company, email, phone number, solar-site location, approximate acreage, facility type, service frequency, vegetation condition, requested service, desired schedule, project description, and any files you choose to attach.

How We Use Information

AgriSolar LLC uses submitted information and attachments to review the request, communicate with you, understand the potential project, and maintain business records related to the inquiry.

Storage and Access

Quote requests and attached files are stored in the AgriSolar LLC Firebase project. Access is intended for the approved administrator. Website infrastructure and email providers may process information as needed to handle website inquiries.

Sharing

AgriSolar LLC does not publish information submitted through the quote form. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when needed to allow service providers to handle your requested communication.

Retention and Requests

To ask about information submitted through the site, contact info@agrisolarllc.com or call (618) 539-2098.

Security

Reasonable technical controls are used for the website and inquiry process, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the website or inquiry process changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.

Questions About This Policy?

Email or call us with your question.
