Frequently Asked Questions

Basic information about services, project fit, and requesting a quote.

What services does AgriSolar LLC provide?

Primary services include commercial solar-farm mowing, targeted vegetation and herbicide management, native planting, erosion-control support, and site-maintenance reporting.

Can you mow an overgrown solar site for the first time?

First-time mowing for overgrown sites can be discussed. The quote depends on vegetation density, terrain, debris, access, panel clearance, acreage, and other site conditions.

Are herbicide applications licensed?

AgriSolar LLC team members hold commercial pesticide licensing. Any contracted application is planned around the target vegetation, label directions, weather, and site requirements.

Do you provide electrical inspection or solar-panel repair?

No. AgriSolar LLC’s scope is vegetation and ground management. Maintenance reporting does not constitute electrical inspection, engineering, performance analysis, or electrical repair.

Where do you work?

AgriSolar LLC is based in Belleville, Illinois. Availability is confirmed by project rather than through a published fixed radius. Send the site location and requested scope for review.

What information is needed for a quote?

Please provide your contact information, company, solar-site location, approximate acreage, requested service, desired timing, and a description of current conditions.

Can reporting be included?

When included in the scope, reporting can cover vegetation observations, selected before-and-after photographs, access-condition notes, and communication about problem areas.
