Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our work and getting started.

What services do you offer?

We provide solar-farm mowing, sheep grazing, vegetation and herbicide management, native planting, erosion-control support, and site reporting.

Can sheep replace all mowing?

Some areas may still need mowing or other vegetation control. Gates, equipment pads, wet or steep ground, and plants sheep leave behind may need separate attention. We review each site before recommending grazing.

Can you help with an overgrown solar site?

We review overgrown sites for a first cleanup. Plant height and density, debris, terrain, access, and panel clearance all affect the work and price. Photos help us understand what is needed.

Are your herbicide applicators licensed?

Team members hold commercial pesticide licenses. Applications follow product labels and account for weather, the plants being treated, and the site’s requirements.

Do you inspect or repair solar panels?

No. Our work covers vegetation and ground care. Site reports do not include electrical inspections, engineering, performance analysis, or electrical repairs.

Where do you work?

We consider sites within a few hours’ drive of Belleville, Illinois, including Southern Illinois, the Metro East, the St. Louis region, and parts of eastern Missouri. Larger or recurring jobs farther away are reviewed individually. See our service area for details.

How do you choose equipment for a site?

We look at panel height, row spacing, gates, terrain, plant growth, and the work requested. Visit Safety & Equipment for more about planning around solar infrastructure.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Start with the site location, the work you need, and your preferred timing. An acreage estimate and photos are helpful. The quote form walks you through the details.

Can I receive photos and a work report?

Yes, reporting can be included in the agreed work. It may cover before-and-after photos, vegetation conditions, access issues, and areas that need attention.

Call (618) 539-2098, email info@agrisolarllc.com, or send a quote request.
