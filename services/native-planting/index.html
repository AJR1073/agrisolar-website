Planting for Ground Cover and Habitat

Native planting can help hold soil in place and support pollinators. The seed mix needs to suit the site and its planting requirements.

  • Native grasses and flowering plants
  • Planting for pollinator habitat
  • Mowing while new plants become established
  • Ground cover to help stabilize soil
  • Work coordinated with the facility’s vegetation plan

Care After Seeding

Before planting, we discuss site preparation, timing, follow-up mowing, and how to handle weeds. AgriSolar LLC can share observations as the new growth becomes established.
