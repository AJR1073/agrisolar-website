Native Planting

Planting and establishment support for vegetation plans that call for native species.

Establishing Purposeful Ground Cover

Native planting can support soil stabilization, pollinator habitat, and a site-specific vegetation plan. Species, seed mixes, timing, and preparation must be selected for the location and project requirements.

  • Native grasses and flowering species
  • Pollinator-habitat objectives
  • Establishment mowing
  • Soil-stabilization support
  • Coordination with the site’s vegetation plan

Establishment Takes a Plan

Planting is only the beginning. Site preparation, timing, establishment mowing, weed pressure, and follow-up expectations should be defined as part of the scope. AgriSolar LLC can discuss the work requested and communicate observations during the establishment period.

Discuss a Native Planting Scope

Share the site location, acreage, existing conditions, and any seed or habitat requirements already established for the project.
