Treating Weeds Where They Cause Problems

AgriSolar LLC team members hold commercial pesticide licenses. Herbicide work is performed by licensed applicators who follow product labels and facility requirements.

  • Invasive plants and noxious weeds
  • Growth along fence lines
  • Weeds around equipment pads and access areas
  • Treatment coordinated with mowing or planting
  • Records and updates for the agreed work

Weather, ground conditions, and the plants being treated help determine whether and when to apply herbicide.
