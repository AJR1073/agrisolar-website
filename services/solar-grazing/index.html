Is Your Site a Good Fit for Sheep?

Before recommending grazing, AgriSolar LLC reviews the site’s vegetation, layout, and animal-care needs.

Why consider grazing

Where Grazing Can Help

Under-Panel Reach

Sheep can reach vegetation beneath panels where larger equipment has limited access.

Less Mechanical Mowing

Grazing can reduce the amount of mowing needed on suitable parts of a site.

Land in Agricultural Use

Sheep allow suitable solar land to support agriculture alongside electricity production.

Before sheep arrive

What We Review Before Grazing

We check the following before recommending grazing:

  • Secure perimeter fencing and usable gates
  • Reliable water access
  • Panel clearance and protected cabling
  • Amount and type of plants available for grazing
  • Poisonous plants, debris, and site hazards
  • Wet areas, steep ground, and drainage
  • Emergency, veterinary, and daily access
  • Facility rules and operating schedules

Areas That Still Need Other Work

Gates, equipment pads, invasive plants, and wet or steep ground may still need mowing, trimming, or targeted herbicide treatment.
