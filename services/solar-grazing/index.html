Livestock-based vegetation management

Solar Grazing for Compatible Solar Sites

Planned sheep grazing beneath and between solar panels, supported by mowing and targeted vegetation work where the site calls for it.

A Practical Tool for Vegetation Control

Solar grazing uses sheep to manage plant growth within an operating solar facility. Their size and grazing behavior can make them well suited to vegetation beneath panel rows and in areas where equipment access is constrained.

AgriSolar LLC builds the scope around the facility—not a one-size-fits-all grazing formula. Vegetation pressure, forage, panel clearance, fencing, water, terrain, weather, and site operating rules all affect the plan.

Why consider grazing

A Different Way to Reach the Ground Beneath the Array

Under-Panel Reach

Sheep can graze beneath and between panels in areas that may be difficult or time-consuming to reach with larger equipment.

Integrated Maintenance

Grazing can reduce some mechanical work while mowing and targeted treatment remain available for areas animals cannot manage effectively.

Working-Land Stewardship

Compatible solar land can continue supporting agricultural activity while vegetation is managed around energy infrastructure.

Site compatibility

What We Review Before Grazing

A grazing proposal starts with practical questions about the facility and the animals’ care. Important inputs include:

  • Secure perimeter fencing and usable gates
  • Reliable water access
  • Panel clearance and protected cabling
  • Forage quantity and plant species
  • Poisonous plants, debris, and site hazards
  • Wet areas, steep ground, and drainage
  • Emergency, veterinary, and daily access
  • Facility rules and operating schedules

Integrated, Not One-Size-Fits-All

Solar grazing does not automatically eliminate every mowing or herbicide need. Some sites and problem areas are better handled with a combination of grazing, mechanical mowing, trimming, and carefully targeted vegetation treatment.

For general industry education, see the American Solar Grazing Association’s solar grazing overview. AgriSolar LLC evaluates each facility and proposed scope individually.
